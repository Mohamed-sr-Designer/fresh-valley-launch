The idea

Egyptian Class A, lived at home.

Fresh Valley · Launch book03
What changed

Seven house rules.

  1. No sizes, anywhere.No millimetres, no grams per piece, no calibre — on the site, the catalogue, the packaging and social.
  2. No zoom.No magnifier, no lens circles, no cropped close-ups. Every product is shown whole.
  3. Whole produce only.Nothing cut, halved, opened or peeled. Ever.
  4. Grade A.The grade reads “A” everywhere.
  5. Lifestyle first.Egyptian people, natural skin texture, linen, villas and compounds, natural light.
  6. The map is the brand.The Valley Map — light contour lines and the towns our produce comes from — replaces fruit photos on every package.
  7. Bilingual.English headline and an Arabic line on everything we make.
Fresh Valley · Launch book04
The pattern · The Valley Map

Eight valleys. One table.

خريطة الوادي — خطوط كنتور خفيفة وأسماء البلاد اللي جاي منها المحصول

Instead of drawing fruit, we draw where it comes from. Thin contour lines of a valley, printed light in one colour, with pins for the towns we buy from: Ismailia, Beheira, Siwa, Qalyubia, Minya, Assiut, Nubaria, Matrouh. It is ours alone, it costs one ink on kraft, and it tells the story without a single fruit photo.
